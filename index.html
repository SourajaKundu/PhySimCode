Reading the results

      

        
Law validity
Are the stated laws scientifically correct, regardless of the video?

        
Law match
Do those laws match the ones demonstrated in the input video?

        
Physics match
Does the regenerated video show the same physics? Rated by people.

        
Parameters recovered
Ground-truth parameters both named and estimated within ±20%.

        
Code runs
Inputs for which the generated code executes without errors.

      

    

    

      Additional metrics Appearance, video similarity & code quality
      
Video similarity measures appearance and can miss incorrect dynamics. It is not a substitute for the human physics ratings above. Higher is better in every column.

      

    

    

      Evaluation setup Sampling, judges & parameter matching
      

        
Evaluation set. 162 experiments × 15 uniformly random samples (seed 42). Models receive the engine name and an experiment-agnostic code template. Parameters are sampled from physically plausible ranges.

        
Law scores. Ratings from 1–5, averaged over three LLM judges: Gemini-2.5-Flash, Grok-4.1-Fast and GPT-5-Nano. Overall law validity ranges from 4.27 to 4.93, while overall law match ranges from 1.51 to 2.47.

        
Human scores. Mean ratings from four expert raters on 100 randomly selected samples per model, on a 1–5 scale.

        
Parameter recovery. Parameters are matched using aliases and Greek-letter abbreviations after unit normalization, then checked within ±20% of ground truth. Scene metadata, geometry, color and g are excluded.

      

    

  






  

    

      
03 / Frontier modelsA closer look at the hard cases.

      
Four newer models join the original ten on a selected set of difficult simulations. Claude Fable 5.1 leads law matching and parameter recovery; GPT-6 Astra and Kimi K3 lead the two video-similarity metrics.

    

    
10 hard samples · 5 SciPy + 5 PyBullet. This is a separate, hand-picked evaluation set. Scores are not directly comparable with the 2,430-video benchmark above.
